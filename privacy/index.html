Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

Freedom is a personal finance progress app from Yakon Design. It helps you track financial commitments, savings goals and pension progress.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Freedom iPhone app, related web experiences hosted by Yakon Design, and when you contact us for support.

Two kinds of information

It is important to distinguish:

  • Financial information stored on your device — such as commitments, savings goals, pension progress, notes, repayments and contributions you enter in Freedom. This stays on your device. Freedom does not operate cloud financial accounts, and Yakon Design does not remotely access those on-device financial records.
  • Personal information processed by Yakon Design or service providers — for example when you email support, when Apple and RevenueCat handle Premium purchases and entitlements, when you visit this website, when fonts are loaded, or when you enable optional PWA push reminders. Those activities are described below.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Freedom, your device may store:

  • Financial tracking information you enter, such as commitments, savings goals, pension progress, notes, repayments and contributions
  • Preferences such as companion selection, onboarding progress, reminders settings and Demo Mode

This information stays on your device unless you choose to export a backup, use device-level backup features provided by Apple, or send information to us yourself (for example by email).

Exports and backups

You can export password-encrypted backups. Freedom does not store your backup passwords. If you forget your backup password, Yakon Design cannot recover the contents of that export for you.

On iPhone, Freedom also maintains a local recovery copy within its iOS-protected application storage. That recovery copy is not separately encrypted by Freedom.

Apple device backups (for example iCloud Backup or computer backups) may also include Freedom app information according to your Apple settings. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by a Freedom cloud account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions under Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

RevenueCat helps manage subscription entitlements. In doing so, RevenueCat may process an anonymous SDK identifier and related purchase, transaction and device information needed to recognise your Premium status.

Freedom does not require a name or email address for Premium, and Freedom does not send your financial records to RevenueCat.

You can read more here:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Reminders

iPhone reminders in Freedom are scheduled locally on your device.

Optional PWA push reminders may use Cloudflare to process technical push subscription information needed to deliver those reminders. Reminder content does not include personal financial figures or Journey/Goal names.

Website hosting and fonts

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting the site may involve ordinary technical request information being processed by GitHub as the hosting provider.

This website loads fonts through Google Fonts. Those requests may expose ordinary technical information such as IP address and device/browser details to Google as the font provider.

The Freedom iPhone app uses locally bundled fonts and does not contact Google to load them.

Advertising and analytics

Freedom currently has no advertising trackers and no third-party product analytics SDKs.

Support communications

If you email freedom.support@icloud.com, we use the content of your message and your email address to respond to your enquiry.

Please do not email sensitive financial information, backup passwords, or other secrets. We only need enough detail to understand and help with your request.

Why we process information (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, the following explains the main processing that involves Yakon Design or service providers — not the financial records that remain only on your device.

  • Managing Premium entitlements through Apple and RevenueCat (anonymous SDK identifier and related purchase, transaction and device information as needed for Premium status) — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / steps at your request to provide Premium).
  • Optional PWA push reminders you enable — technical push subscription information processed via Cloudflare to deliver those reminders — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps necessary to provide the reminder feature you requested).
  • Hosting this website via GitHub Pages — ordinary technical request information — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in operating and securing the Freedom website).
  • Loading fonts via Google Fonts on this website — ordinary technical request information that may be exposed to Google — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in presenting the website consistently).
  • Responding to support emails — your email address and the content of your message — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests in responding to enquiries about Freedom). Where your message relates to an existing subscription or contract with us, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR may also apply.

Local scheduling of iPhone reminders and local storage of your financial information and preferences take place on your device as part of how Freedom works. Yakon Design does not remotely access those on-device financial records.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including the GDPR), you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and rights related to data portability. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may have the right to object. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent (Freedom does not currently rely on consent as a primary legal basis for the processing described above).

Because most Freedom financial records are stored on your device, you can edit or delete individual records while Freedom Premium is active. If your subscription expires, your existing information remains available for viewing. You can remove Freedom’s locally stored information at any time by deleting the app. Exported backups remain under your control.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission: https://www.dataprotection.ie.

Retention

Financial records and preferences remain on your device until you edit or delete them in the app (where those controls are available), or until you delete the Freedom app from that device.

Turning off reminders in Freedom cancels scheduled iPhone reminders created by the app. Deleting the Freedom app also removes those on-device reminder schedules with the app. Exported backups you created, and Apple device backups, may remain separately under your control until you delete them.

Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any related follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law.

Subscriptions and uninstalling Freedom

Deleting (uninstalling) the Freedom app removes Freedom’s locally stored information on that device. It does not cancel an Apple subscription. Subscription cancellation is managed through your Apple Account subscription settings.

International processing

Some providers that help deliver Freedom or this website — such as Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, Google (for website fonts) and Cloudflare (for optional PWA push) — may process technical or purchase-related information in countries outside Ireland or the EEA.

Details of how each provider handles international transfers are set out in that provider’s own privacy documentation. This policy does not claim that any particular transfer mechanism has been independently verified for every provider. Please review the privacy information published by Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, Google and Cloudflare for more detail.

Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to Freedom’s design — including local-first storage of financial records and optional password-encrypted exports. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Device security, backup passwords you choose, and Apple account security remain important parts of protecting your information.

Children

Freedom is not directed at children, and Yakon Design does not knowingly collect personal data from children for Freedom. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us (for example by email), please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page. Where changes are material, we will take additional steps to notify you as appropriate.
