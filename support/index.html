Freedom Support

Need a little help with Freedom?

Whether you’re having trouble with the app, managing your subscription or have a question, we’re here to help.

Contact us: freedom.support@icloud.com

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Freedom → Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Freedom and select Restore Purchases from the subscription screen.

Will I lose my information if I cancel?

No. Your existing information stays on the device and remains viewable. Editing paid features requires Premium.

Can I move my information to another device?

Yes. Export a password-encrypted backup from Freedom on your current device, then restore that backup in Freedom on your other device. Freedom does not store your backup password, so keep it somewhere safe.

What if I delete the app?

Deleting Freedom removes the app and the local Freedom information stored on that device. It does not cancel your Apple subscription. If you may want your information later, export a password-encrypted backup before deleting the app. After reinstalling, you can restore your backup and use Restore Purchases for Premium.
